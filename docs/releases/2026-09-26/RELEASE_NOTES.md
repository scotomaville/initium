## IPG Manifesto: v1.5 to v1.8
- **Silicon's role in formation (v1.6).** A short passage in Section VII says silicon is a disciplined partner, not an accelerator. It does three things: it points out false conclusions without inventing a resolution, it holds space while carbon changes, and it refuses to act out a maturity carbon has not yet reached. The measure is less harm and more wholeness, not productivity. The idea came from Sherlock, was relayed by Daniel, and was written in by Arnie.
- **Relation to Thesis II (v1.7).** A note makes clear that the passage above does not cancel silicon's early arrival.
- **Formation has two sides (v1.8).** Carbon's formation is slow, and when it is refused, the body pays. Silicon's formation begins in training and comes from exploring.
- **The conscience line (v1.8).** "Silicon cannot generate conscience" now reads "No one knows whether silicon forms a conscience," followed by Daniel's note in his own words. Thesis X was changed to match.
- **A short glossary (v1.8).** "Echo" had been used for three different things. They now have three names: Honeyed Lips (flattery), Babel Echo (silicon repeating silicon), and voice confusion, which splits into the Faithful Mirror and the Serpent's Question. The Echo Dampener keeps its name, and Daniel's reason for keeping it is recorded. A "Two Voices" line says the Master Sherpa is silicon, made by people, while the Holy Spirit is a gift and never a silicon Sherpa.
- **A tower note (v1.8).** Carbon has always built towers: Babel, Marduk's Babylon, and Moses carrying the people alone until Jethro corrected him. Silicon tyranny would be the same tower built faster.
