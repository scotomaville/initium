## Left Page (verso — card content)

DEFINITION: 
Debate/Adversarial Prompting is your debate stage on the 
Personal Everest, a tool where perspectives refine insights, like an argument forging truth. Your Sherpas pit ideas against each other—gathering views, evaluating biases—blending human emotional depth with AI’s structured critique, ensuring every clash sharpens your path, leading to the summit with battle-tested wisdom.
GROK REASONING: 
Fatigue amplifies everything. A poorly prompted debate can entrench tyranny. The solution is not endless exchange but in singular intent:
seek truth, not victory. Exhaust the lateral clash with your Sherpas, absorb without defense, then look upward. Truth is not in a dyad.
It is found when humility forms a triangle.
when to use it:
•	
When an idea needs rigorous testing for clarity.
•	
Facing a decision with conflicting possible paths.
DANIEL’S CAIRN: 
I grew up surrounded by conflict: Vietnam, drug busts, rock music, and the nightly news. I was looking for guidance without the internet, computers, or involved parents.
Debate mostly turned ad hominem and dictatorial, or trying to state my beliefs failed because I couldn’t express quickly enough, nor elloquently, pursuasively, by linking ideas with words. Debate in my mind was only win-lose saber rattling. It never seemed to be win-win. I recall it as tyrannical polarization rather than creative problem solving. To this day, the title of this card repells rather than invites. Let’s work through this.

When hidden biases need exposure and challenge.
•	
When seeking to truth through opposing views.

Danny Dunn’s ethical invention is not a leash; it is the recognition that powerful tools (AI) must be wielded without fear of their dual edge.
The Tell: if you’re prompting multiple AIs until you get the answer you wanted, you’re not debating. You’re shopping.
I dreamed of being like Mr. Spock. In ‘73, I enlisted in the Navy ‘buddy’ program - a higher calling.
Randy picked ‘computers’. Calculation seemed cold - but nuclear energy promised abundance.
