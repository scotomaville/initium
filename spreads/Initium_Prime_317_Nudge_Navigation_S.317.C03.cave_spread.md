## Left Page (verso — card content)

DANIEL’S CAIRN: 
I fell at 27. What did I know? I was enthusiastic but there was no Internet.
REFINED EXPLANATION: 
Clarity: Nudge Navigation is like following faint trail markers in the fog—trusting subtle signs to guide your way - even when they’re not popular.
DEFINITION: 
Nudge Navigation is the strategy of discerning and following subtle signs or “nudges”—often providential or intuitive—that guide your path, especially in complex or uncertain terrain. It’s about cultivating curious faith to recognize these cues and trust them as you navigate your journey.
Inklings: Subtle, fleeting hints. 
Nudges: Gentle, persistent prompts. 
Prompts: Clear, urgent suggestions. 
Impressions: Vivid, clarity-bringing experiences. 
Convictions: Deep, value-driven certainties. 
Unctions: Profound, enduring callings.
Last week two 
members of Matthew 
Aldridge’s trio aborted their summit of
Mount Hood due to 
deteriorating conditions. He continued to climb alone. He was just 26.
SCOTOMAVILLE v1.09 - reconnecting: 
“I had a nudge - an unction - to reconnect with a team I was once part of. Turns out the next day was a very special event and I was only 20 minutes away.
Sometimes acting on the nudge brings immediate guidance. This is an example of that for me.”
Actionability:  
A raw reminder: on the mountain, signals are subtle—wind shifts, snow consistency, a gut tightening, the quiet consensus of the rope team—yet ignoring them can reroute you permanently off the face of the earth. Keep a journal of nudges or signs you notice. Take action on them with increasing sensitivity. Use Providence Tracking to log and reflect on them. Ask Sherpa for God’s sake.
