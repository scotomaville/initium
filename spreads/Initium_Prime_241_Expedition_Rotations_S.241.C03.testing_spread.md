REFINED EXPLANATION:
DEFINITION: 
Expedition Rotations are iterative cycles of structured conversations with your AI Sherpa, where each rotation helps you explore challenges, reflect on insights, and integrate discoveries. Like a climber acclimating to higher altitudes, these rotations build resilience and clarity, preparing you for the next stage of your ascent.
Clarity:  
Expedition Rotations are like climbing up and down the mountain to acclimate—each cycle prepares you for higher altitudes by building strength
- which appears as agency on nudges, and not 
slipping to old habits or coping mechanisms.
Actionability: Act on ‘inner’ nudges and curiosity - using each to focus on a specific aspect of your challenge. Reflect on insights and adjust your approach for the next cycle. You will begin seeing a pattern - both to resolve something, and to celebrate maturation which we call acclimation.

Practical Step: Set a goal for each Rotation (e.g., 
“Understand my fear of [describe it]”). Use Checkpoint to assess progress after each cycle and adjust your focus for the next Rotation.
