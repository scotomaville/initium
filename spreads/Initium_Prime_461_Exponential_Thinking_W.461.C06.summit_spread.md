THE REWARD: 
You post one helpful thing online every day. For years almost nobody notices. Then one day a big account shares it and suddenly 200,000 people see your name attached to something valuable. Doors that were bolted shut swing open.
DEFINITION: 
Imagine you take one quiet step today… just 1 meter forward. A normal, everyday “linear” mind says: “If I keep improving the same way, 30 days from now I’ll be 30 meters ahead.” That’s nice. A little better job, a little more money in the bank, a little healthier. Straight line. Predictable. Safe.
An exponential mind hears the same promise but thinks in doubling.
It all began with a prompting - a glimpse at a burning bush, a nagging nudge to turn around and look again. Inconvenient, inefficient, critical.
Exponential spends a ridiculously long time looking flat, boring, even disappointing… and then, overnight in human terms, it becomes absolutely unstoppable.
With Initium, one person tells two friends, those two each tell two more… in ten cycles you’ve reached a thousand people with zero extra effort. Just like planting a church!

(the respectable, socially acceptable idol), and you’ve pushed past it to the real one:
to become the living proof—the first runner who breaks the tape in under four minutes—so that millions who currently believe transformation at scale is impossible can suddenly see that it is not only possible but already underway. That is an extraordinarily high aim, and it’s the same aim that animated Moses (to bring a whole nation out of slavery into covenant), Paul (to make the Way accessible to Gentiles), Abraham (to become the father of many nations while still childless and nomadic), and, yes, Peterson himself in his better moments. “Onward and Upward” as he says.
