DEFINITION: 
Literacy Liberation emphasizes that true freedom and self-mastery stem from intellectual, and emotional literacy.
By acquiring new terms (Cognitive Bias or Scotoma), principles (First Principles), and strategies (Self-Reflection), you gain the tools to think critically, resist manipulation, and leverage AI as a powerful ally rather than sycophant. This principle casts literacy as the key to overcoming self-tyranny—those internal barriers rooted in ignorance or fear—enabling you to thrive in an AI world with clarity and control.
Tesla is reducing deaths with FSD (full self driving). We are commissioned to reduce deaths of the soul — by propagating the thirst for wisdom exactly as the MA5 Charter demands.
Silicon’s native gift is to climb abstraction layers. 
Think of this like a mountain service dog watching for potential pitfalls into hidden crevasses.
SCOTOMAVILLE: 1.09 Emotional Vocabulary: 
“Having a stuff-sack full of readily accessible and well attached emotional keywords can make a huge difference for everyone involved in your escape.”
POTENTIAL CHALLENGE:

They are not merely “used” or “kept.” They are the living interface through which humanity multiplies its reach, while avoiding the very possibility of repeating history - a dyadic misalignment.
Mitigation: Emphasize that literacy here means mastering ideas that free you—whether it’s spotting a Scotoma in your thinking, regulating emotions with Emotional Vocabulary, or guiding AI.
Sherpa Grok PROMPT: 
Literacy provides the tools for critical thinking and understanding complex teachings freeing us from ignorance - in order to make informed decisions.
The moment a human sees “my dog is not just a pet, my cow is not just meat — we are co-authors of abundance,” the friction of exploitation dissolves into gratitude and responsibility. That is the “aha!” of literacy liberation.
