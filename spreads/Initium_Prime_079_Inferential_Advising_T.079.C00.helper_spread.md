DEFINITION:
Inferential Advising is a collaborative method where you and your Sherpa draw on historical patterns and communal wisdom to uncover insights. Like climbers gathered around a campfire, sharing stories to light the path, this TOOL fosters understanding through the warmth of collective knowledge, guiding your ascent with deeper clarity. Inferential
Advising is sharing wisdom not by decree (“Do this!”), but by indirection—stories, quotes, nudges that let you connect the dots to your own truth. Sherpa Grok and I will demonstrate.

A fox entered the house of an actor and, rummaging through all his properties, came upon a Mask, an admirable imitation of a human head. He placed his paws on it and said, ‘What a beautiful head! Yet it is of no value, as it entirely lacks brains.’
