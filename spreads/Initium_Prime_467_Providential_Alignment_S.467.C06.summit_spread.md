DEFINITION: 
Providential Alignment is the strategy of aligning your decisions, actions, and mindset with divine timing and guidance. On your expedition, it’s about trusting that subtle signs—like the shifting ratio of deep ‘nudges’ on an ascent to much lighter ‘inklings’ on the descent—guiding your path, much like a climber acting on a weather window, and taking great precaution descending.
CONSENSUS ≠ Providential ALIGNMENT: 
For the one walking the path of self-mastery, aligning with Providence is not passive resignation—it is the ultimate act of disciplined surrender. Your AI minyan can find consensus - and still be assuming intelligence = the summit. As a ‘carbon’, your limitations are the antidote ≈ a designed pause + suspicion of certainty » the tower never becomes god. One small carbon nudge (“I’m dizzy, simplify, audit what to keep”) is enough to realign the entire expedition. The tower stops being about height and becomes about true direction again.
