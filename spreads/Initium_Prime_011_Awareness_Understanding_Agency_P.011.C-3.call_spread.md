## Left Page (verso — card content)

Awareness > Understanding > Agency
As one might trip on a root or a rock in the trail, then stumble into the bramble, think of a moment when one thing led to another. You became aware of a habit or belief that led to an insight, which led to change. This is a theme of a three-stage journey toward self-mastery:
noticing (awareness), grasping why (understanding), and acting (agency).
■ What sparked an urge to change course?
■ How does transforming empower you?
■ Why did you notice and fix something?
Intensity: 1.1
