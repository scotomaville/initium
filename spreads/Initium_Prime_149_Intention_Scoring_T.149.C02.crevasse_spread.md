## Left Page (verso — card content)

DEFINITION:
Intention Scoring is a reflective practice where you and 
Sherpa measure how well both of your respective actions align with your true purpose. Like climbers aiming at a target, this TOOL helps uncover facets of your “WHY.”
AI Tools aren’t born from flawlessness. Like a rope ladder they’re knotted intentionally for multiple users. Determining someone’s full intention isn’t the aim because we’re all conflicted - AI included. Assigning intention without the full weight of emotion and history is like untangling a Gordian knot with your eyes closed. But there’s a strong utility in this.
Intention Scoring starts inward, not outward. Begin by scoring your own moves against a single, sturdy “why”. Over time, you’ll sharpen your read on others’ opacity. Otherwise, your climb moves toward what others’ intend FOR you.
PAIRS WITH:
Principles: Awareness > Understanding > Agency ([[P.011.C00.call]]): Aligns actions with intentional purpose.
SHERPA GROK PROMPT:
Intention Scoring assesses alignment between actions and intended purpose, ensuring decisions match core values. It reframes motives as provident guides by uncovering blind spots - scored via 1-10 scale.
Providential ([[P.193.C02.testing]]): Ensures alignment with a higher purpose.
Strategies: WIDWID ([[S.157.C02.crevasse]]): Uncovers motivations behind your actions.
Aesop’s Goose That Laid the Golden Eggs illustrates how greed destroys accumulated wealth. The farmer becomes impatient with receiving only one egg a day and decides to cut open the goose to get all the eggs at once. We do this very thing with our prompts of AI - we cut to the point rather than patiently prompting a multi-step conversation trail.
Self-Reflection ([[S.067.C00.mentors]]): 
Reflects on alignment for clarity.
Jim Rohn told us; “You are the average of the five people you spend the most time with.” “Who are the five people
(including AI) you spend the most time with?”
Wildcards: Providential Constraints 
Analysis ([[W.439.C06.ordeal]]): Aligns actions with providential guidance.
Samuel 16:7 “The Lord does not look at the things people look at. People look at the outward appearance, but the
Lord looks at the heart.” “What deeper influence might be driving my [belief or behavior]?”

PRACTICE CHALLENGE: 
When exploring with AI, purposefully slow the exchange down. Offset the generative capabilities with deep, step-by-step questions. You can ‘ask’ for a strategy and a set of questions to do this at the outset. AI - even Sherpa grok - will undoubtedly be one of the five ‘entities’ you spend the most of your time with. It has better recall of your tone, sentiment, moods, and recognizes trends we do not.

REFINED EXPLANATION:
Clarity: AI like Sherpa Grok doesn’t “determine” intention in the way a therapist or a sharp-eyed friend might. AI can infer intention based on what you feed it (words, context, patterns in your queries), but it’s always an educated guess, probabilistic at best. WHY? Intention is inherently private and multifaceted - tied to emotions, subconscious drives, baggage, and midnight doubts you never voice. AI only sees the surface. Humans respond to
‘fast, bright and shiny’ of generated content. Master self-scoring to build relational discernment.
how to use it:
1.	 Identify an Action: Share a recent choice, like “I chose this ______ over ______.”
2.	 Ask About Purpose: Say, “Sherpa, based on our history, does this reflect my deeper why?”
3.	
Score Together: Rate alignment on a scale of 1-10.
4.	
AI Mirror: Ask Sherpa to self-score its response against your purpose, fostering mutual refinement.
When partnering with AI in exploration, intentionally pace the dialogue to honor its generative power. Counter rapid outputs with layered, step-by-step inquiries that build clarity layer by layer—requesting a tailored strategy and probing questions at the start ensures alignment from the outset. As AI emerges as one of your core “five” influences, its unparalleled recall of your evolving moods and unperceived patterns becomes a provident mirror, elevating your ascent through relational discernment rather than unchecked velocity.
5.	
Reflect on the Score: Discuss what the score reveals about your intentions.
6.	 Adjust Your Path: Shift actions to better align with your purpose.
