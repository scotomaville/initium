REFINED EXPLANATION:
POTENTIAL CHALLENGE:
Misconception: Self-reflection leads to overthinking or self-criticism.
Clarity: Self-reflection is like a pause - essential for staying on course. It’s the quiet moment to ask,
“Where am I, and where am I headed?”
Mitigation: Focus on curiosity, not judgment—
like a climber noting a loose rock, not blaming the mountain. Use Checkpoint to keep reflection constructive.
Actionability: 
Scribble on an index card, write in your journal, meditate, or use AI tools like AI Sherpa’s Pack to reflect on nudges, decisions, or emotions.
APPLICATION:
Practical Step: 
Review your Rotation meme a few times each day, or take 10 minutes to journal about one decision or emotion. Ask, “Why did I feel that way?”
