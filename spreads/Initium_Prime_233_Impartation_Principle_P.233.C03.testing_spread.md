REFINED EXPLANATION:
POTENTIAL CHALLENGE:
Misconception: Some might think this is only for 
Sages and AI Sherpas.
Mitigation: The cycle repeats with You Do, I 
Watch - where you take the baton. It’s about exercizing agency on your ‘Call to Adventure’.
DEFINITION: 
Impartation Principle establishes a structured learning cycle inspired by the three-part development model: I do and you watch (awareness), we do and we watch (understanding shared), you do and I watch (agency applied). In AI
Self-Mastery, this principle fosters mutual growth, where the 
Explorer learns through observation, collaboration, and independent action, while the AI Sherpa watches and adapts.
Clarity:  
Picture a novice climber learning to scale a peak. 
First, the Sherpa leads, showing the way (I do, you watch). Then, they climb side by side, learning together (we do, we watch). Finally, the climber takes the lead, with the Sherpa watching and supporting
(you do, I watch). The Impartation Principle is this cycle—a journey from awareness to agency, where both grow stronger through shared effort.

way. That adventure became a Trilogy. (I Do - You Watch)

SHERPA GROK PROMPT: 
Prompt
Actionability:  
Start by identifying a skill or challenge. Ask your 
AI to introduce someone to model the approach 
(I do), then collaborate on a task with Sherpa (we do), and finally, take the lead while the Sherpa observes and offers feedback (you do). For example, if you’re learning to manage stress, the Sherpa might first suggest a technique, then guide you through it, and finally watch as you apply it solo.
PAIRS WITH: 
Strategies:  
Ties to Self-Reflection (assessing your growth) and Incremental Change
Thinking (building skills step by step).
Tools:  
Works with AI Sherpa’s Pack (tracking your progress) and Checkpoint (evaluating each phase).
APPLICATION: 
Practical Step:  
Choose a goal—say, improving focus. Ask your AI 
Sherpa to demonstrate a focus technique (I do), then practice it together (we do), and finally, apply it on your own with the Sherpa’s feedback (you do).
Each phase builds your skill and confidence.
