REASONING: 
CoT is iterative: start with a simple premise, apply reasoned steps repeatedly, anticipate consequences at each link, and build toward clarity/ order. This mirrors fractal generation — no grand design upfront, but disciplined repetition uncovers profound structure. Self-mastery arises from this practice: by externalizing the “chain” (making thoughts visible and inspectable), we gain control over complexity, avoid blind leaps, and cultivate deeper insight into our own reasoning processes.
DEFINITION: 
Chain of Thought is like following footsteps to a landmark. 
Your Sherpa guides you, step by step, linking thoughts. In conversation, it’s a journey where human intuition and AI logic intertwine, leading us to the summit with deliberate, truth-aligned strides. CoT turns hidden assumptions and shortcuts into visible, inspectable steps. You can pause, backtrack, and correct errors mid-chain. Over time, repeatedly externalizing your reasoning trains stronger internal habits — better intuition, fewer blind spots, and greater command over your own mind. It is disciplined thinking made audible, and therefore improvable.
