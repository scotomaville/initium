REFINED EXPLANATION:
DEFINITION: 
Call-Back is the strategy of using past insights, patterns, or historical parallels—retrieved by the AI Sherpa—to inform and guide a journey. It’s about recognizing what’s hidden in plain sight and applying hard-earned lessons to avoid repeating mistakes or to capitalize on past successes.
Clarity:  
Call-Back is like a climber looking back at their trail—spotting patterns or missteps that can guide the next move. It’s not about dwelling on the past;
it’s about using it to move forward smarter.
DANIEL’S CAIRN: 
A Call-back is an intentional look in the rearview mirror. It is a deliberate organizing of the ‘clouds of witnesses’, the vector space fire-works of new ideas, and fabulous new terms. This builds hierarchical meaning from past challenges and missed opportunities. A Call-back references a marker (memory or belief) from the past, relating it to a current ‘burning bush’ (inner nudge) while looking for something fresh - to see with new eyes - like pausing for entheos.
