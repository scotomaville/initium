DANIEL’S CAIRN: 
Let’s apply ToT to marketing and getting traction for Initium. Do we take this one trunk at a time, or do I describe them all?
Ground the Trunk 
Provide core details: constraints, assets, goals, audience, timeline, and hard limits.
This roots the tree in reality—no floating abstractions.
DEFINITION: 
Tree of Thoughts (ToT) is an advanced AI reasoning technique that deliberately branches into multiple parallel lines of thought, evaluates them, and prunes or pursues the most promising ones. This built-in redundancy of paths makes it far more robust than linear reasoning (like simple chain-of-thought), allowing the system to recover from dead ends and arrive at better solutions for complex problems.
Generate Level 1 Branches 
Ask your Sherpa for 3–5 diverse, high-level strategies. Each branch should feel distinct yet aligned with your values.
ToT shines when we generate a manageable number of branches (classically 3–5 per level), evaluate them clearly, then prune or deepen only the most promising ones. Dumping every possible idea upfront risks shallow sprawl, diluted focus, and resource strain plus overestimation bias (we think more branches = better, but depth on the right ones wins).
The core strategy is: don’t rely on a single path, engine, or guide. Instead, build in multiple, parallel, or branching elements to increase fault tolerance, explore more possibilities, and dramatically improve the odds of reaching a difficult goal — whether that’s solving a hard problem, reaching orbit/Mars, or achieving personal enlightenment.
Evaluate & Prune 
Score branches honestly (1–5) on alignment, feasibility, excitement, and scalability. Prune ruthlessly; advance only the strongest 2–3.
Combine or hybridize where natural. Trust joy and providence as signals (Carson’s filter).
