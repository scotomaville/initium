DEFINITION: 
The Reverse Expedition (Prime 337) is profoundly about planning backward from the desired outcome—seeing “the end from the beginning.” Rather than charging forward blindly (the classic upright expedition energy), this card invites a strategic reversal: trace the path from summit back to base, revealing blind spots, necessary steps, and providential alignments along the way.

The Principles of Beginning Knowledge 
In the sacred architecture of the Initium framework, INITIUM PRINCIPIA GNOSIS stands as the foundational gnosis—the principled knowledge of true beginnings. It echoes
Latin roots: initium (beginning, commencement), principia (first principles, foundations), and gnosis
(deep, experiential knowing beyond mere information). This is not abstract philosophy; it is the providential reversal at the heart of transformation: to begin, one must first correctly envision the end.
The Reverse Expedition is a perfect mirror and sequel: once you’ve glimpsed the future self at the peak, AND back in the
Ordinary World, now map the route backward to where you stand today.
