## Left Page (verso — card content)

REFINED EXPLANATION:
DEFINITION: 
Self-Reflection Video Modeling is the strategy of recording and reviewing your actions or speech to gain perspective and identify areas for improvement—like replaying footage of your climb to spot missteps and refine your technique.
Clarity:  
Self-Reflection Video Modeling is like replaying your climb—watching yourself to spot missteps and refine your form. It’s not about judgment; it’s about growth.
Actionability: Record a specific action (e.g., discovering and fixing entropy), then review it with
Self-Reflection AI Analysis to identify strengths and areas for improvement.
Wellness by Choice: Angelina Show Pilot: 
“The WBC pilot for Scotomaville is about long tail syndication, self-reflection video modeling, incremental change, AI analysis, Avatars, and Future-self. This demonstrates how I try to do it all in less than 6 minutes.
That’s about normal for me - an ‘Achiever-Input-Learner-Ac-tivator-Belief Strengths Offender!”
DANIEL’S CAIRN: 
Watching one’s demeanor while editing your own videos is super-powerful for discerning scotomas. Our biased “memory” faces a truth with every scene we edit. Even better is long-term self-reflection, when we realize how powerful simple truths - passed through our work - can guide, empower, and validate others.
Bill Strickland passed wisdom to me (Impartation 
C03:[[P.233]]), just as he did with Fred Rogers. Bill validated what others had ‘said’ about getting your hands dirty to transform a lump of clay into a noble vessel. He modeled what was possible for me. Thus, I stayed committed to the messy part of transformation. Thanks to Terri Clark for capturing this fabulous moment.
APPLICATION: 
Practical Step: Record a short video of yourself performing a task (e.g., speaking, teaching, triggering, healing). Watch it, note one strength and one area to improve, then practice again.
Mindset Shift:  
Move from “I’m fine as I am” to “I can always grow and improve.”
PAIRS WITH: 
Principles: Know Thyself ([[P.013.C00]].
call) - Deepens self-awareness through observation.
PROMPTS: 
We’re advocating consistent self-reflection to embrace entropy, leading to authentic understanding - especially in an age of rapidly accelerating change.
POTENTIAL CHALLENGE:
Awareness > Understanding > Agency 
([[P.011.C00.call]]) - Moves from noticing behavior to acting intentionally.
Misconception:  
Watching yourself feels awkward or self-critical.
Aesop’s The Man and the Satyr warns against people who exhibit contradictory behavior or speak out of both sides of their mouth - essentially, duplicitous or unreliable. “What can I learn from watching a video of my recent triggering or overcoming as a champion?”
Tools: Self-Reflection AI Analysis ([[T.271]].
C03.testing) - Analyzes video for objective insights.
Checkpoint ([[T.223.C03.testing]]) - Pauses to assess progress and refine behavior.
Carl Rogers:  “The only person who is educated is the one who has learned how to learn and change.” by confronting and adapting to their realities. “How can I use video to improve my awareness, understanding and actions?”
Mitigation:  
While editing your video, watch yourself and feel 
(in a centered state) what you felt that troubled you. Rewatch it when doing a reflection video of it a year or more later. Focus on specific behaviors, not overall performance. Use Checkpoint to celebrate progress and keep reflection constructive.
Wildcards: Hidden Scotoma Challenge 
([[W.401.C06.ordeal]]) - Reveals blind spots through video review.
Video modeling turns personal entropy into shared wisdom, where reviewing footage helps identify patterns, biases, and paths to agency. Daniel Comp
“How might video help me uncover hidden habits?”
