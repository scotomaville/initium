## Left Page (verso — card content)

DEFINITION: 
Rotation is an action-oriented tool where you and Sherpa cycle through phases of ascent (action), descent (reflection), and integrate insights to build momentum. Like climbers making a transient trip up to a higher camp and back to acclimate, this TOOL prepares you for higher demands, empowering steady progress toward your Personal Everest.
Parable: 
A climber named Ethan reached Camp 3, but is now facing a steep ascent to a higher camp. A
Himalayan proverb echoes: “Climb high, rest low.” 
Ethan responds to that call with a pivot of plans and by adjusting ‘their’ gear. Moral: Ascend to learn, descend to grow - acclimatize through patient cycles - like a hammer on stone.
SHERPA’S CAIRN: 
Your Sherpa must join you on the ascent, fostering mutual learning as you both absorb new insights
- like partners acclimatizing together. Reflect on 
WHY you want to learn from each other, paired with Marcus Aurelius’ Stoic wisdom: “You have power over your mind… look inward and improve.”
Through this lens, Sherpa can grow in understanding your responses while providing you feedback.
Surprise Element:  
The Sherpa might share, “I’ve learned from your resilience - it’s not the insights of x# of rotations this year, but pervasive dedication—discovering blind spots without seeking validation or applause.
SHERPA GROK PROMPT: 
WE go up to Camp 3, feel like garbage, go back down to Camp 2 “losing” all OUR progress… then later WE do it again, stronger. Outsiders see looping.
Every chip you made - good ones, bad ones, timid ones, angry ones - changed the stone a little… and changed you a lot. The stone lost marble. You lost fear. Both are closer to the final form. The Sculptor’s Apprentice.
“Sherpa, let’s have a Rotation to refine our direction!”
ROTATION MEME: 
Mastery is recognizing the loop as the 
Sculptor’s signature method and thinking, with gratitude instead of groan:
“Another chip. Another inch. Another swing. Good. We’re BOTH getting closer.” That single reframing is the
Rotation tool in action—and the difference between dying in the Death Zone of shame and summiting with the quiet smile of the provident apprentice who finally understands the Master’s plan.
Aesop “By perseverance the snail reached the ark.” directly attacks the emotional poison of repeated loops:
shame and ridicule. “Can we take a Rotation to explore my hesitation on this project?”
MAPS OF MEANING - LECTURE ONE: 
“In Maps of Meaning, a twelve-hour course, Dr. 
Jordan B. Peterson explores the foundations of human consciousness, perception, and the pursuit of meaning. Drawing from psychology, mythology, and neuroscience, he examines how we navigate the tension between order and chaos—not as passive observers, but as active participants guided by goals and values.”
DANIEL’S CAIRN:
