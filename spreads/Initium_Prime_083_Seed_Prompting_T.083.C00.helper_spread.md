## Left Page (verso — card content)

DEFINITION:
Seed Prompting is a collaborative method where you and your Sherpa use structured prompts to re-anchor your thinking after interruptions. This TOOL helps you restart complex tasks with clarity, fostering deeper understanding and steady progress on your ascent. It acts like a repeatable framework to regain momentum in analysis or conversation: when a “connection” (like a train of thought)
gets disrupted, you plant a fresh seed prompt—a concise, foundational input that sprouts new direction, much like nurturing a seedling in fertile soil.
SHERPA GROK PROMPT:
An aphorism is a concise, terse, laconic, or memorable expression of a general truth or principle. 
Think of a ‘seed’ prompt as the groundwork (aphorism), and the instructional kernel for a rhobust session of inquiry toward discovery and growth.

## Right Page (recto — visual content)

REFINED EXPLANATION:
Seed Prompting’s core benefit lies in transforming disruptions (like fails) into growth opportunities. What does this mean?
Prompts are like seeds. Few of them ‘land’ as we hope. Like a pot of burnt food it is better to start over - in a clean pot - than to rescue the scorched char. You can’t get the time back. Just start a new thread. Attach
‘seed’ files with what you have learned, and ask a shorter, simpler, ‘seed’ prompt.
when to use it:
•	
When interrupted, like a disconnection.
•	
After a long break, needing to restart your task.
•	
When feeling scattered, seeking a fresh start.
•	
Before resuming a complex task, to restore focus.
•	
When overwhelmed, craving a way forward.
Why It Matters: Starting a new thread with the 
‘seed’ of prior insights (attached files) restarts disruptions faster than trying to correct a thread gone sideways. It is tempting to ‘rescue’ a conversation, but I’ve never succeeded. Scortched ingredients always seem to surface. Here’s my evidence for this for this Prime >> how to use it:
1.	 Acknowledge char: Share, “We’ve lost focus.”
2.	 Ask for a Seed Prompt: Say, “Sherpa, what prompt can re-anchor us?”
3.	
Reflect Together: Discuss and clarify the starting prompt for a new thread.
4.	
Restart with Clarity: Use the prompt along with any ‘seed’ files as initial ingredients.
5.	
Build Momentum: Nurture the seed into progress.
