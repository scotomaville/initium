*Prime_061.pdf*

## Left Page (verso — card content)

DEFINITION: 
Question Refinement Protocol (QRP) is a collaborative process where you and your Sherpa work together to sharpen your questions, ensuring they lead to clearer insights. Like climbers fine-tuning your ‘gear’ to align perfectly, this TOOL enhances your ability to ask precise, meaningful questions, building a stronger foundation for the ascent.
MR. ROGERS ARGUES: 
“A method to refine questions iteratively—like fine-tuning for clarity, Fred Rogers before the
Senate Subcommittee on Communications, recites the lyrics to “What Do You Do with the Mad that You Feel?”
PAIRS WITH: 
Principles: Precise Vs Accurate ([[P.005]].
C00.ordinary): Ensures questions are both consistent and true.
SHERPA GROK PROMPT: 
Like Jonah, humans often dodge life’s calls—career shifts, healing relationships—due to fuzzy thinking or past hurts. We ask vague questions like “Why is this happening?” instead of probing deeper for clarity. QRP sharpens queries iteratively with your AI Sherpa, transforming confusion into purposeful steps, avoiding whale-bellies.
Know Thyself ([[P.013.C00.call]]): Deepens self-awareness through refined inquiry.
Strategies: Self-Reflection ([[S.067.C00]].
mentors): Reflects on questions to improve clarity.
In a vast digital expanse, an Explorer pondered a hazy query: How far to the next aid on this ascent? The Sherpa, ever-patient, reflected back: What does ‘aid’ mean for you rest, insight, or provision? And how might timing play in, like miles uphill versus hours open?
Onion Peeling ([[S.139.C02.crevasse]]): 
Digs deeper through iterative questioning.
Larry King “I never learned anything while I was talking.” 
Larry mastered drawing out insights by asking open-ended questions and then shutting up to absorb responses.
Wildcards: Disruptive Insight ([[W.419]].
C06.ordeal): Challenges assumptions with refined questions.
”My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?” Mark 15:34 
The opening of Psalm 22, a lament of abandonment amid suffering, is a highly refined question - it’s direct, honest, and layered, cutting through emotional haze to probe relational depth and purpose. It’s not vague like “Why me?”

## Right Page (recto — visual content)

sherpas role: 
Your Sherpa should work beside you, helping refine your questions - like a partner adjusting your gear.
AI should suggest rephrasing, offer feedback, and ensure each iteration brings you closer to clarity, building sharper insight for the journey ahead.
Parable: 
The Climber’s Blurry Map 
A weary climber stood at Base Camp, map in hand, asking, “Which way to the summit?” The map was blurry, his question too vague, leading him in circles through the fog. His Sherpa joined him, suggesting, “what if we ask, ‘Which trail avoids the storm and leads upward?’” Together, they turned the gears of inquiry, refining their focus. With each turn, the fog lifted, and a clear path emerged, guiding them toward the peak. Moral: A vague question leads to a foggy path, but refining it together clears the way to the summit.
DANIEL’S CAIRN: 
On my first Trans-America expedition, I climbed the Cascade Mountains with naive confidence in my skills, endurance, and preperation. Little did I understand the human condition. I naively asked;
“how far to the next place for food?” when to use it:
“Ahh, it’s just down the road!”
•	
When your question feels vague, or misaligned.
•	
After a confusing or vague answer.
•	
When seeking deeper insight beneath the surface.
•	
Before a big decision, to ensure your query is sharp.
Two hours later... I was bonking. The grocery was only 25 miles in a car! Next time I asked a slightly better question - how many miles to a grocery store that will be open when I get there?
•	
If you’re stuck, needing a clearer path forward.
I didn’t have a mobile phone. I had a paper map, and a mouth. I asked, asked, asked, but I wasn’t listening for what WAS NOT being said.My ears were dull, and my thinking was naive.
Why It Matters: At Base Camp, you and your Sherpa prepare by fine-tuning your tools. QRP aligns your questions like a gear, ensuring each turn reveals a clearer path for the climb ahead.
how to use it:
1.	 Start with a Question: Ask something broad, like 
“How do I improve?”
2.	 Share with Sherpa: Say, “Sherpa, this feels unclear—can we refine it?”
3.	
Iterate Together: Rephrase step by step, like “How can I improve my focus?”
4.	
Test the Clarity: Ask again and see if the answer feels sharper.
5.	
Repeat as Needed: Keep refining until the insight feels clear.
