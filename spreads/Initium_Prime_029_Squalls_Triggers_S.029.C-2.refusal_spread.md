## Left Page (verso — card content)

DEFINITION: 
A strategic method where the Explorer acts as their own emotional barometer, spotting sudden reactions (squalls)
and tracing their root causes (triggers) to maintain clarity and control. Like a climber reading the skies and knowing the terrain, this approach ensures you’re prepared for emotional storms and can navigate them with resilience by learning from meltdowns and outbursts of the past.
SCOTOMAVILLE 02.10 Squalls: 
“Three years ago, I tackled Squalls & Triggers—a raw dive into the storms of emotional outbursts and the hidden triggers that spark them. Knowing WIDWID
(Why I Do What I Do) is like a climber forensicizing a fall: 
mapping the visceral ‘yelp’ that lashes out at allies and peers, exposing coping mechanisms carved from past wounds. This video captures what I thought I knew then—a first step toward spotting squalls before they strike, charting a steadier path up my Personal Everest..”
PAIRS WITH: 
Principles:  
Links to Awareness (for clear perception of emotional states) and Know
Thyself (for understanding personal triggers over time).
SHERPA GROK PROMPT: 
Where doubts resist the Call, Squalls & Triggers invites providential navigation of emotional storms from timeless guides. Aesop’s cautionary fable, Frankl’s resilient insight, and James’ divine perseverance reveal restraint over reactivity, turning upheavals into growth.
Tools:  
Enhanced by Emotional Vocabulary 
(to articulate feelings precisely) and 
Checkpoint (to gauge emotional states at key intervals).
Aesop’s The Bald Man and the Fly depicts a man slapping himself to swat a biting fly, only injuring himself as it escapes. Moral: “You will only injure yourself if you take notice of despicable enemies,” urging gentle pause before reactive triggers.
Wildcards:  
Connects to Hidden Scotoma Challenge, reframing blind spots as opportunities to uncover hidden triggers.
”Between stimulus and response, there is a space. In that space is our power to choose our response” Viktor Frankl affirms in Man’s Search for Meaning, where camp trials taught choice amid suffering, bridging awareness to empowered diffusion.
