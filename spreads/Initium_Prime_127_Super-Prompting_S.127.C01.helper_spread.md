## Left Page (verso — card content)

REFINED EXPLANATION:
DANIEL’S CAIRN: 
Brian Roemmele’s Professor SuperPrompt:
Clarity: Super-Prompting is like plotting a climb—
each question is a foothold toward clarity. It’s not just asking; it’s asking how to ask smarter.
DEFINITION:
Super-Prompting is the art of crafting precise, layered questions to extract valuable insights from AI, reducing bias and enhancing clarity—like plotting a climb where each question is a step toward the summit of understanding.
Actionability: 
Start with “What’s the best way to approach this?” and refine to “What are the three most effective strategies for this specific challenge?
Structured Reasoning: 
Builds logical, layered questions.
AI Self Mastery Promo:
“Migrating from classic ‘search’ with short keyword strings toward more targeted and complex questioning is a skill developed through many dumb prompts. This video demonstrates the output of super-prompting reasonably well - though indirectly.”
Self-Reflection: Encourages you to think critically about your queries.
AI-Human Collaboration: Harnesses AI’s power through precise, intentional prompting.
Sherpa Grok PROMPT:
Super-prompting on self mastery is a strategy for self-dialogue, habit formation, goal achievement, and overcoming mental barriers. Let’s talk about it!
PAIRS WITH:
Principles: 
First Principles ([[P.101.C01.mentors]]) 
- Breaks down complex queries into 
fundamental truths.
The Fox and the Stork “One bad turn deserves another.” 
Aesop uses mismatched meals to highlight tailored communication, reframing vagueness as empathy opportunities.
APPLICATION:
Practical Step: 
Before hitting the ‘Enter’ key asking AI a question, re-read your prompt, and then refine it twice each time making it more specific and layered. By adding a tone, a background, and an aim, you’ll see the quality of AI’s response improve.
Truth Over Comfort ([[P.019.C00.call]]) - 
Prioritizes clarity over validation in AI interactions.
Dale Carnegie (How to Win Friends and Influence People) “Ask questions instead of giving orders.” Carnegie’s questioning fosters collaboration, reframing orders as inquiries.
