  At Camp 1, days and decisions can feel scattered even when they belong to one life. Think of a
  stretch of recent events that still reads as noise. Narrative Arc asks you to notice beginning,
  challenge, and turning points in what you are already living—not to invent drama, but to recognize
  which chapter you are in. Meaning often shows up when the sequence is seen whole. You do not need
  a finished ending to know the shape so far.
