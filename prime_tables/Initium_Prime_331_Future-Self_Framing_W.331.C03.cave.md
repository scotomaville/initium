  Still in Agency’s Approach, hesitation often hides a refusal to picture who you would have to
  become. Think of a future self six months or six years out whose life would require different
  habits starting now. Future-Self Framing uses that concrete vision to activate agency—writing the
  aim plainly enough that today’s choices can answer to it. Goals matter because they shape the
  person required. See the future clearly enough to act today.
