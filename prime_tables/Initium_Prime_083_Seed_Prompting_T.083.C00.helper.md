  Even at Base Camp, an interruption can wipe the working context you were holding. Think of a task
  you lost mid-thread—a conversation, a draft, a plan—and the fog that followed. Seed Prompting is a
  structured way to re-anchor with a clear restart so the work can continue instead of starting from
  nowhere. You are not recovering lost hours by wishing. You are planting enough context to grow the
  next stretch of focus.
