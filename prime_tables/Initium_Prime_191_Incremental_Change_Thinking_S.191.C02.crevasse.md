summary: >-
  At the First Threshold, waiting for a dramatic breakthrough can hide the quieter damage of delay.
  Think of a skill, relationship, or practice that has barely moved because each day asked for only
  a small step you skipped. Incremental Change Thinking builds through consistent small improvements
  that compound—while neglecting the same size of step can compound the other way. Patience here is
  active, not passive. Choose the next small move you can actually repeat.
sherpa_version: Incremental Change Thinking's taking baby steps - like learning to walk little by little.

  with Hill’s persistence and Romans’ renewal.
