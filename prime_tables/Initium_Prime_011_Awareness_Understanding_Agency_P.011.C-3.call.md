summary: >-
  Noticing is not the same as understanding, and understanding is not yet a move. Bring to mind
  something you’ve seen clearly enough to name, but haven’t yet grasped—or grasped, but haven’t
  acted on. Awareness > Understanding > Agency asks where you are in that sequence right now, and
  what the next honest step would be without skipping ahead. The sequence is a direction, not a
  badge. Choose a slope to locate yourself—not to force a leap to agency.
sherpa_version: Awareness > Understanding > Agency's spotting stuff, getting it, then acting on it - like seeing a hill, knowing
  how to climb, and not quitting.
reasoning: This principle uncovers blind-spots in passive observation, reframing stages as a growth ladder. A Providential
  nudge from the Ugly Duckling fosters purpose, linking awareness to action. It escalates from spotting issues to grasping
  meaning, driving agency with Rogers’ process and Philippians’ work.
expert_high: Luke
expert_high_quote: 'A son leaves home, falls into wasteful habits, realizes his mistakes, and returns to a welcoming father
  - showing awareness opens new paths. Biblical Parable: The Prodigal Son (Luke 15:11-32)'
expert_high_reason: Luke’s Prodigal Son progresses from lost awareness to repentant agency and homecoming, reframing failure
  as divine redemption. As a physician-evangelist, Luke’s gospel highlights compassionate transformation in human stories.
  Links Rogers’ process to Ugly Duckling. Supports Maslow’s growth-to-transcendence and Bloom’s evaluating choices, nudging
  divine purpose.
expert_medium: Carl Rogers
expert_medium_quote: The good life is a process, not a state of being. It is a direction, not a destination. (On Becoming
  a Person, 1961)
expert_medium_reason: Rogers’ person-centered therapy guides clients from awareness to agency through self-acceptance, reframing
  stagnation as a directional process toward better living. His psychological work, based on empathetic client interactions,
  emphasized growth’s fluidity. Links Ugly Duckling to Luke’s return. Supports Maslow’s growth-to-transcendence and Bloom’s
  creating change, nudging purposeful progress.
expert_low: 'Hans Christian Andersen: The Ugly Duckling'
expert_low_quote: It is only with the heart that one can see clearly. What is essential is invisible to the eye - but in time,
  the duckling sees his reflection, understands his swan nature, and flies with grace.
expert_low_reason: The Ugly Duckling’s journey from rejection to swan identity reframes difference as evolving self-understanding
  and graceful agency. Andersen’s tale, inspired by his own awkward youth and unaccepted talents, celebrates hidden potential.
  Links to Rogers’ process. Supports Maslow’s belonging-to-growth shift and Bloom’s understanding identity, nudging purposeful
  acceptance.
