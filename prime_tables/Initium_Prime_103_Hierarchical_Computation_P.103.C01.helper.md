  Camp 1 understanding rarely appears as one dump of facts. Think of a body of information you have
  gathered that still will not support a clear next move. Hierarchical Computation asks you to stack
  knowing in layers—from raw material toward ordered insight—so each level can hold the next.
  Stewardship here means building only as far as the last layer will bear. Do not confuse a pile
  with a structure you can climb on.
