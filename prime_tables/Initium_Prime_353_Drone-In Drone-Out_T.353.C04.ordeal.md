  In the Ordeal, stuckness often comes from staying at one wrong distance. Think of a problem you
  have either buried in tiny details or lost in a vague overview. Drone-In Drone-Out shifts
  perspective on purpose—close inspection, then wide reframing—so the stuck point can be seen both
  ways. Some things are in your control; many are not. Alternate the zoom until a usable next move
  appears.

  into diminishing terrain on a greater ascent.'
reasoning: Drone-In Drone-Out alternates close inspection of stuck points with wide-angle reframing, revealing blind spots
  as opportunities for providential insight. Beginning with Epictetus’ circle of control to restore agency amid chaos, it
  ascends through Aurelius’ cosmic detachment to shrink emotional threats, and culminates in John’s vision of prepared rooms
  - transforming financial precarity, aging entropy, and legacy anxiety into waypoints on a guided ascent. This layered shift
  turns limitations into understanding, fostering resilient action and deep peace.

  shift and Bloom’s creating breadth, nudging insightful expansion.
