summary: >-
  Base Camp awareness can stop at recognizing facts without being able to use or create from them.
  Think of a skill or subject where you can recall terms but stall when asked to apply, analyze, or
  make something new. Bloom’s Taxonomy names that ladder—from remembering toward creating—so you can
  see which rung you are actually on before calling it mastery. Formal storage of knowledge is not
  the same as self-education that climbs. Locate the rung; then take the next honest step.
sherpa_version: Bloom’s Taxonomy charts cognitive growth from recall to creative work.
reasoning: This principle finds blind-spots in rote knowledge, reframing taxonomy as cognitive peaks. A Providential nudge
  from the Path of the Just sparks ascent, turning recall into creation. It escalates from noticing levels to comprehending
  innovation, enabling action with Rohn’s education and Solomon’s wisdom.

  and Bloom’s evaluating wisdom, nudging providential mastery.
expert_medium: Jim Rohn
expert_medium_quote: Formal education will make you a living; self-education will make you a fortune.
expert_medium_reason: Rohn’s self-education for fortune reframes learning as a ladder from recall to creation. From farm roots

  shift and Bloom’s creating fortune, nudging innovative wisdom.
