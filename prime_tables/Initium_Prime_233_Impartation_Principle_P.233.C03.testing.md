  Camp 3 is a poor place to learn alone when practice can be shared. Think of a skill or
  understanding that grows faster when someone teaches beside you—and when you teach in return.
  Impartation Principle is collaborative learning through shared practice: what passes between
  people gathered for the work, not only what one person studies in isolation. Mentorship here is
  mutual enough to leave both changed. Offer and receive the practice.
