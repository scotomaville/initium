summary: >-
  At Camp 2—Knowing WHY—a setback can look like the end of the usable path. Think of an obstacle,
  failure, or blocked plan that still feels fixed when you replay it. Reframing asks what changes if
  you alter the perspective rather than the facts alone: the same ground, a different reading that
  may open a next move. Curiosity helps more than special talent here. You are not asked to pretend
  the setback was pleasant—only to test whether another frame fits better.
