  Camp 3 can keep you busy while purpose drifts. Think of actions that look productive and still
  feel oddly empty or off-true when you ask why they matter. Purpose Alignment Modeling debugs what
  you are doing against deeper meaning—so bias, habit, or borrowed goals can be corrected before
  they harden. Humility helps when pride would rather not look. Recalibrate the compass; then
  resume.

  maturity and Ecclesiastes’ eternity.

  pondered life’s deeper meaning. Links Paul’s maturity to Phantastes. Supports Maslow’s growth-to-transcendence and Bloom’s
  synthesizing meanings, nudging providential alignment.

  letters debugged faith for churches. Links Phantastes to Ecclesiastes’ heart. Supports Maslow’s cognitive-to-growth shift
  and Bloom’s evaluating issues, nudging aligned clarity.
expert_low: Phantastes (1858, George MacDonald)

  nudging refined purpose.
