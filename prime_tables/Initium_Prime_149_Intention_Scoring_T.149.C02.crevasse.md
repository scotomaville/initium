  At the First Threshold, busy action can hide a drifted purpose. Think of a commitment, habit, or
  project that still looks active while no longer matching what you intended or value. Intention
  Scoring asks you to measure that gap before more effort is spent on the wrong route. Alignment is
  the mechanism—not speed. Pause long enough to score the match, then decide whether to continue,
  adjust, or stop.
