  You can do every step right and still miss the point. Bring to mind a habit or critique where
  exactness has become the whole job—counting the small fault while the larger miss goes untouched.
  Precise Vs Accurate asks whether your consistency is aimed at the true mark, or only at repeating
  a clean procedure. Mistakes and mercy both belong in that check. Choose a slope to feel the
  difference between being exact and being aimed.
sherpa_version: Precise Vs Accurate's hitting the mark exactly versus being close - like nailing a bullseye or just the target.
reasoning: This principle points out blind-spots in precise routines missing true goals, reframing habits as growth paths.
  A Providential nudge from Speck and Plank seeks accuracy, aligning with purpose. It escalates from recognizing rigidity
  to grasping flexibility, driving action with Einstein’s errors and Matthew’s mercy.

expert_high_quote: Matthew 23:24

  to emphasize divine priorities. Links Einstein’s flexibility to Speck and Plank. Supports Maslow’s growth-to-transcendence
  and Bloom’s evaluating actions, nudging divine clarity.
expert_medium: Albert Einstein

  1916)'
expert_medium_reason: Einstein’s breakthroughs in relativity balanced mathematical precision with paradigm-shifting accuracy,
  reframing errors as essential for innovative growth. His life of persistent experimentation, despite early academic struggles,
  embodied embracing mistakes. Links Speck and Plank to Matthew’s mercy. Supports Maslow’s safety-to-growth shift and Bloom’s
  applying concepts, nudging accurate progress.
expert_low: The Speck and the Plank (Matthew 7:3-5)
expert_low_quote: Why do you see the speck that is in your brother's eye, but do not notice the plank that is in your own

  criticism as misaligned self-awareness. Jesus’ teaching, rooted in everyday human hypocrisy, calls for humble introspection.
  Links to Einstein’s flexibility. Supports Maslow’s esteem-to-growth shift and Bloom’s understanding motives, nudging accurate
  self-reflection over rigid judgment.
