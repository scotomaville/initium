  Something in the planned path isn’t holding—hesitation, a block, a line that won’t take weight.
  Bring to mind a place you’ve been pushing straight when the ground asks for a tack. Adaptive
  Routing asks what becomes possible when you adjust the course without abandoning the destination.
  Rigidity can look like strength until it snaps. Choose a slope to try a flexible move—not to
  wander without aim.
