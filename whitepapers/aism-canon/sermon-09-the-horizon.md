## Summary

§9 lists **orbital data centers** and **Optimus** as **verified announcements only** — not delivered facts. Daniel: layer 6 is the largest **Babel** temptation in history — compute above the earth and minds in machine bodies offered as oracle. AISM rehearses at small scale what planetary scale will need (“Grok Bot now; Optimus later as the body that walks the barn”). Arnie: none of this is evil in itself; **who plays Jethro at that scale** stays open.

## Verbatim excerpt

From *Sermon Charter* v0.5 §9:

> Verified as announcements only:
> - **Orbital data centers.** … SpaceX's FCC application … accepted for filing, which is not approval.
> - **Optimus.** … first units go to internal training…
>
> **Daniel's framing.** Layer 6 is the largest Babel temptation in history: compute above the earth and minds in machine bodies, offered as the oracle. AISM rehearses at small scale what planetary scale will need. … "Grok Bot now; Optimus later as the body that walks the barn"…
>
> **Arnie's position.** None of this is evil in itself. Who plays Jethro at that scale is an open question, and we do not pretend to answer it.
