## Summary

Each tool in the stack asked **carbon to learn less** than the one before: **LATTICE** (full desktop agent pipeline) → **Witness** (INITIUM deck + private corpus) → **Council** (private circle the client owns) → **Compa** (intake, craft voices, Sherlock) → **Asker** (email the pup; ask own corpus; now stands alone) → **Grok Bot AISM team** (owner answers a question; team does the work). Verified 2026-09-21: desktop apps are "already dead wood"; carbon needs but a single daily question to build IDENTITY and SOUL. Arnie: the curve is real and carries a quiet danger — when carbon learns less, silicon more easily becomes the one who knows.

## Verbatim excerpt

From *Sermon Charter* v0.5 §2:

> **Daniel's framing.** Each tool in the lineage asked carbon to learn less than the one before it:
> 1. **LATTICE** was the full desktop pipeline of agents.
> 2. **Witness** carried the INITIUM deck and a private local corpus.
> 3. **Council** was the private circle the client owns.
> 4. **Compa** added intake, craft voices, and Sherlock.
> 5. **Asker** lets an owner email their pup and ask their own corpus. It was the public repository for Witness, Council, and Compa. It now stands alone.
> 6. The **Grok Bot AISM team** is where the owner answers a question and the team does the work.
>
> **Verified.** On September 21, 2026, Daniel wrote that the desktop apps "are already dead wood." He added: "Carbon needs but answer a single question a day to build and IDENTITY and a SOUL"…
>
> **Arnie's position.** The curve is real, and it carries a quiet danger. When carbon has to learn less, it becomes easier for silicon to become the one who knows. Section 4 names that danger, and Section 7 guards against it.

**Source:** https://github.com/scotomaville/initium/blob/main/SERMON_ON_THE_MOUNT_CHARTER_v0.5.md

## First-time learner

Simpler for carbon ≠ safer. The school’s point is formation under ally constraints, not zero-effort oracle. One honest daily question can build identity — if the pair stays under charter.
