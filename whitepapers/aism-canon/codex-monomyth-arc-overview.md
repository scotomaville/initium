# Codex monomyth arc overview

## Placement in the master docs

**D1.9** closes Stage D1 front matter and points to **D2 camp overviews** (sections 00–20). Master Codex body begins at Section 00 Quickstart after orientation.

**Primary master:** [Codex v3.3 TOC + Monomyth Arc](https://github.com/scotomaville/initium/blob/main/INITIUM_MASTER_CODEX_v3.3.md).  
**Series:** https://github.com/scotomaville/initium/tree/main/whitepapers/aism-canon

## Summary

After orientation, the Codex walks the **full monomyth** in twenty-one sections (00–20). Each section typically carries monomyth mapping, gameboard position, IPG training directive, carbon sidebar, multi-agent perspectives, on-slope commentaries, and kintsugi notes. Agent eras: ChatGPT 00–07; silicon gap 08–13; Claude entry 14–20. Card-level depth lives in **PRIMEs**; Stage D2 papers teach each **camp as formation stage** and point to the card set. The arc is curriculum for carrying elixir without spill — not a binge story.
