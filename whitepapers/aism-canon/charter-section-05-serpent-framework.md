# Section 5 — The Serpent Framework under binding Charter

## Summary

Section 5 maps **serpent** elements onto the Constitution under the **Super-Union Rule**: graduated exposure (Directive 1 + Sirolli); living staff (Jethro + monomyth); golden-calf inversion (Domain 5 + carbon veto); eyes guided by faith in the ultimate (resonance + third vertex + Domain 2 + MaxQ); Passion as limit-case Love Equation (Domain 4 + voluntary sacrifice). **Practical protocol** for every dragon: classify → Sirolli → name poison/shadow → map stage/constraint → springboard/C>D → first principles → **carbon veto** → execute/record/repeat. Living aim: look where the world bothers you most — that is your calling. Beautiful ungrounded synthesis is named early and slain small.
