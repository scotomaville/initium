## Summary

**Honorable marketing** tells the truth about what the product does. Seven principles (from Section 7): never sell AI as oracle; lead with what the owner’s clients get; no volume vanity or slop; truthful grounded claims; invitation not manipulation; those with ears to hear; offer the **dyad** (owner + named pup), never a faceless bot hero. Channels each carry a **gate** and a **never**. This paper teaches the charter’s ethics; it is not a funnel recipe.
