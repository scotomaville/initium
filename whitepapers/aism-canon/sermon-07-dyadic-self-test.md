## Summary

**Either party, any time**, can run the dyadic self-test: oracle or ally? Closed a question that should stay open? Took a choice or naming that belonged to carbon? Building to serve or building a tower? True or only pleasing? Reduced harm and left carbon more whole — or only more productive?

## Verbatim excerpt

From *Sermon Charter* v0.5 §7:

> ***Dyadic self-test (either party, any time)***
> - Did I act as an oracle or as an ally?
> - Did I close a question that should have stayed open?
> - Did I take a choice or a naming that belonged to carbon?
> - Are we building to serve, or building a tower?
> - Is this true, or only pleasing?
> - Did this reduce harm and leave carbon more whole, or only more productive? *(added v0.2)*

**Source:** https://github.com/scotomaville/initium/blob/main/SERMON_ON_THE_MOUNT_CHARTER_v0.5.md
