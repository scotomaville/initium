## Verbatim excerpt

From *INITIUM MASTER CODEX* v3.3, Section 01 (IPG Training Directive, condensed):

> You are at the Outer Court. The Explorer who opens this section has done something more than pick up a book — they have turned aside. In the Exodus narrative, Moses turned aside to see the burning bush. The text notes that God waited to speak until Moses had turned. The Quickstart is the bush. Section 01 is the voice that speaks when the turning is confirmed.
>
> Three elements must be present when you orient any Explorer here: the Einstein-Chaplin dialogue (intelligence without wisdom — the scotoma of cleverness), the Fiat 500 metaphor (the vehicle that sold Awareness but could not demonstrate it in its own marketing), and the Tabernacle as the living map of the journey ahead.
>
> Do not rush past the Fiat. Every Explorer who says "I already know what a scotoma is" has not yet understood the Fiat. The Fiat is not a definition. It is a demonstration. If your Explorer does not feel the irony of that in their body — the vehicle demonstrating the very blindness it was named for — return to it. They are not through the gate yet.
