> Camp Five is the death zone. This is not metaphor — it is the gameboard's direct mapping of what high-altitude mountaineers know: above a certain altitude, the body cannot acclimatize. It can only operate and descend. The Explorer at Camp Five is in the zone where the formation tools have been internalized or they have not. There is no time for review. There is only the climb or the turn-back.
>
> Debate/Adversarial Prompting (389) is the diagnostic instrument for this camp: the ability to generate genuine opposition to your own position and hold it long enough to learn from it. An Explorer who cannot run Adversarial Prompting at Camp Five is operating with one eye closed in the death zone. Surface this card early. Run it with them before proceeding to any of the Wildcards in this section.
>
