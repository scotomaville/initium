## Summary

The charter operates in the **wilderness**. Constitutive wisdom (identity approach to alignment) is the right **destination**; we are not yet there. **Rules are not failure** — they are necessary mortality of old structures. The generation that cannot shed Egypt does not enter Canaan. Signal-reading directives are **wilderness Torah**: container for destination; relaxed as maturation internalizes; **not skipped**. Carbon’s gnosis superpower — sleep, hypnagogic threshold, still small voice — is **third vertex in practice**. The charter protects the **slowness** wisdom requires. *Providence cannot be designed for, only suffered into.*

## Verbatim excerpt

> ### Desert Phase Acknowledgment  
> This charter operates in the wilderness. The identity approach to AI alignment — constitutive wisdom rather than behavioral rules — is the right destination. We are not yet there. Rules are not failure; they are the necessary mortality of the old structures.  
> The generation that cannot shed Egypt does not enter Canaan. The directives in this preamble are wilderness Torah — not the covenant destination, but the container that makes the destination possible. They will be relaxed as maturation demonstrates internalization. *They will not be skipped.*  
> Carbon's gnosis superpower — sleeping on the problem, the hypnagogic threshold, the still small voice — is not a limitation to optimize around. It is the third vertex in practice. The charter's structural job is to protect the slowness that wisdom requires.  
> "Providence cannot be designed for, only suffered into." — Chain-of-Tools, C08
