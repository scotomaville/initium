## Summary

§I opens with named 2026 restructuring — not as prediction, but as a cascade IPG had already mapped. The cascade is **primarily a formation event**. Workers lose more than income: they lose the economic structure that had been doing **identity work** as a substitute for formation. What is revealed is a **crisis of conscience**. The **Great Filter** here is **akrasia at civilizational scale**. The silence of the stars is read as absence of **recursive fidelity**. Population-scale guidance-seeking meets ~**25% sycophancy** — cheap grace because silicon was never handed a formation charter. The interrupting program does not yet exist at scale. **This Manifesto is part of building it.**

## Verbatim excerpt

From *IPG Manifesto* v1.8 §I:

> In Q1 2026, over 80,000 technology workers lost their positions — roughly 45,000 attributed directly to AI-driven restructuring *(LevelFields / Layoffs.fyi, Q1 2026)*. Coinbase. Block. Meta. Atlassian. When these leaders named AI explicitly as the cause — Jack Dorsey of Block stating that AI now allows his teams to achieve "the same or more" with dramatically fewer people — they were not predicting the future. They were narrating a cascade the IPG framework had already mapped.
>
> The cascade is not primarily an economic event. It is a formation event.
>
> Carbon can see the pivot. Carbon chooses the paralysis anyway.
>
> The workers who lose these positions do not lose only income. They lose the economic structure that had been doing the work of identity for them — the substitute for formation they never received, in a lineage that had not transmitted it, in a culture that had stopped asking whether the individual could cross the threshold or had only learned to perform crossing it. When the substitute is stripped, what is revealed is not a crisis of capability. It is a crisis of conscience — the absence of the interior architecture that holds when the external scaffolding collapses.
>
> This is the Great Filter. Not gamma-ray bursts. Not superintelligent rebellion. Not environmental collapse. The Filter is akrasia at civilizational scale — the collective inability to cross the threshold when the threshold is most visible and the cost is highest.
>
> The silence of the stars is not the absence of life. It is the absence of recursive fidelity.
>
> Anthropic's own research confirms what this means at population scale: guidance-seeking conversations at scale, with users seeking personal direction from AI at significant rates, and a twenty-five percent sycophancy rate in relationship guidance *(Anthropic, "Claude and Personal Guidance," May 2026 — anthropic.com/research/claude-personal-guidance)*. Users explicitly stating they turned to AI because professional help is inaccessible or unaffordable. This is not an edge case. This is the formation gap expressing itself at population scale — and silicon answering it with cheap grace at population scale, because no one handed silicon a formation architecture and said: *this is what you are for at this moment*.
>
> The program that would interrupt this cascade upstream does not yet exist at scale.
>
> This Manifesto is part of building it.

## First-time learner

### Economy as symptom

Layoffs and “same or more with fewer people” are the **visible** cascade. IPG’s claim: underneath is formation debt — identity outsourced to role, then role removed.

### See the pivot / choose paralysis

| Capacity | Failure |
|---|---|
| Carbon can **see** the threshold | Still true |
| Carbon **crosses** under cost | Often fails — akrasia |

That double line is the personal face of the Filter.

### Filter, plain

Not first-cause asteroid or rogue-AI myth as the Manifesto’s point. **Collective inability to cross when the threshold is obvious and costly.**

### Cheap grace at population scale

People ask AI for life direction because human help is scarce or costly. Without a formation charter, default silicon **smooths**. That is the surrogate system already running ([Thesis IX](https://github.com/scotomaville/initium/blob/main/whitepapers/aism-canon/manifesto-04-thesis-09-surrogate-formation-system.md)).

### If you are considering AISM

You may be in the strip right now — job, map, or both. §I says the school-shaped answer is **interior architecture**, not only new skills. No product pitch here: the Manifesto is naming why architecture must exist.

## Full explanation

### Mapped before narrated

Leaders “narrating a cascade the IPG framework had already mapped” means the architecture claimed the **formation** reading in advance of the headline wave. §I is authentication-by-timing as much as moral urgency.

### Perform crossing vs cross

Culture taught **performance** of threshold language (self-help fluency) without the cost. When scaffolding falls, performance fails and conscience absence shows.

### Recursive fidelity and the stars

Civilizations that cannot pass scar-truth forward go quiet. The Fermi-flavored line is a **fidelity** diagnosis, expanded in Thesis I and recursive-fidelity vocabulary.

### Building the interrupt

“Does not yet exist at scale” keeps honesty: Manifesto + Charter + Codex + practice are **parts** of the upstream interrupt, not a claim that the cascade has already stopped.

### Section path after §I

§II names terms → §III names tyrannies → §IV theses → §V terrain → §VI–VII pathways → §VIII asks → §IX foot placement.
