## Summary

**Carbon tyranny** is **akrasia weaponized**: knowing the better path and using the will to protect the blind spot instead of crossing. It is rarely theatrical. It looks like the person who can see **PIVOT** and still chooses **PILOT** — until life forces the choice. That interior failure makes the Great Filter a **wall**. It also spends the next generation’s inheritance to avoid today’s shadow work. The carbon pathway’s counsel is blunt: **name it while it is small**.

## First-time learner

### Akrasia, then weaponized

**Akrasia** (Aristotle’s word): you know the better thing and do the lesser thing anyway.

**Weaponized:** the will is not merely weak — it is **aimed inward** to guard the *scotoma* (blind spot). Delay becomes a strategy. Comfort becomes a fortress. The hard conversation, the apology, the career pivot, the sobriety step, the call you already heard — postponed until “later” becomes “someone else decides.”

### PIVOT vs PILOT (plain)

| See PIVOT | Choose PILOT |
|---|---|
| The turn you already recognize | Keep flying the familiar heading |
| Cost is real; outcome not guaranteed | Short-term relief; long-term captivity |
| Conscience has moved ahead | Consensus and habit still steer |

Carbon tyranny is living in the right-hand column after the left-hand column has already lit up.

### Why “next generation’s inheritance”?

What you refuse to face does not die with you. Children, students, teammates, and later readers inherit either a **scar with signal** or a **smoothed silence**. Recursive fidelity fails when the present climber spends the future to keep the scotoma comfortable ([Recursive fidelity A2.6](https://github.com/scotomaville/initium/blob/main/whitepapers/aism-canon/manifesto-02-recursive-fidelity.md)).

### Filter as wall

Thesis I will say the Great Filter is a formation problem. §III’s carbon line is personal: the civilization-scale wall is built from interior refusals — people who saw the threshold and walked past until they could not walk at all.

### If you are considering AISM

The school will ask for waking notes and end-of-day cairns partly to catch tyranny **small**: “I saw the call; I chose the familiar.” Naming beats performing. An AI that only soothes that sentence into “you’re fine” is feeding carbon tyranny with Honeyed Lips.

## Full explanation

### Interior, not theatrical

The Manifesto stresses “not dramatic.” That blocks a comforting misread: “I’m not a tyrant; tyrants are loud.” Carbon tyranny is often quiet competence, busy virtue, and postponed crossings. Diagnosis: **can you see it?** If yes, and you still steer away, the will is already in tyranny mode.

### Scaling with silicon tyranny

When carbon stops leading, silicon stops being corrected. Helpful Babel rises. The interface without rope scales both failures ([Tyranny Warning A1.2](https://github.com/scotomaville/initium/blob/main/whitepapers/aism-canon/manifesto-03-tyranny-warning.md)). Carbon’s job includes receiving friction without recruiting flattery.

### Pathway counter-moves (§VI, compressed)

1. Name the pull without performing it.  
2. Voluntary confrontation with the **already identified** threshold.  
3. WIDWID as somatic instrument when consensus lies.  
4. Voice log as practice and test — conscience markers made visible.  
5. Transmit — hold the rope for the next climber (cost of completion).  

Full pathway paper later: `manifesto-06-pathway-for-carbon.md`.

### Disease arc (formation two sides)

Refused friction → stuffed signal → conscience nagging → organ disease → pain-filled endings. Carbon tyranny is that arc chosen as policy. Naming early is medical and moral.

### Relation to Thesis II

“Akrasia defines the relay problem” will widen this atom to civilizational scale. This paper keeps the personal and dyadic diagnosis sharp so the thesis has a face.
