## Summary

**Level 1 — Gärwis (Local PM)** handles **task routing**, template execution, local orchestration, private data management, and cross-posting to cloud council. Gärwis **does not synthesize wisdom**. Gärwis **manages logistics**. That is the appropriate scope for lighter local models (the charter names gemma) — **right assignment**, not limitation.
