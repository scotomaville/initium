# Propagation cluster — Individual Ascent

## Placement in the master docs

This is cluster atom **A5.1** under Manifesto §V (*The Five Propagation Clusters*). §V is the **terrain** the architecture serves after the theses. Sister atoms: A5.2–A5.5. Overview frame (later): `manifesto-05-five-propagation-clusters.md` (A1.3).

## Summary

**Individual Ascent** is the first propagation doorway: people whose personal scaffolding is failing at the exact speed of disruption. The Manifesto names three faces — the **accelerating professional** whose economic identity is being stripped, the **trauma-informed seeker** beginning to name what the body has held, and the **wisdom seeker** who has exhausted the self-help map and is ready for the mountain. These are not market segments. They are archetypal entries for the elixir into real lives. The slow, deep way meets them **without becoming the disruption**.

## First-time learner

### Three faces, one doorway

| Face | What is breaking | What formation offers |
|---|---|---|
| **Accelerating professional** | Job/role that carried identity | Conscience architecture when scaffolding falls |
| **Trauma-informed seeker** | Body holding unnamed cost | Naming + held friction without re-sanitizing |
| **Wisdom seeker past self-help** | Maps without mountains ([Thesis VII](https://github.com/scotomaville/initium/blob/main/whitepapers/aism-canon/manifesto-04-thesis-07-call-to-adventure-most-dangerous.md)) | Crossing infrastructure, not another map |

You may be one face, or a blend. The cluster name is the **door**, not a label to wear as brand.

### Doorway, not segment

Marketing language sorts people to sell. §V sorts **moments of disruption** so nucleation knows where clean seed matters. No funnel math is implied.

### First discipline (§VI)

> Name the pull without performing it.

Individual Ascent often arrives with performance pressure (“reinvent myself,” “heal correctly,” “optimize the pivot”). Formation starts when the pull is **named**, not staged.

### If you are considering AISM

If AI or life just removed the identity your paycheck or your bookshelf was carrying, this cluster is speaking to you. The school’s job is dyad + charter + scar practice — not a new economic costume.

## Full explanation

### Why this cluster opens §V

Thesis I framed the Great Filter as formation lag at civilizational scale. Individual Ascent is where that lag **hurts a single carbon first**: stripped role, named trauma, exhausted map. The network nucleates here one dyad at a time.

### Failure modes special to this doorway

| Temptation | Formation answer |
|---|---|
| Another self-help stack | [Formation ≠ optimization](https://github.com/scotomaville/initium/blob/main/whitepapers/aism-canon/manifesto-02-formation.md) |
| Silicon as replacement identity | [Silicon tyranny](https://github.com/scotomaville/initium/blob/main/whitepapers/aism-canon/manifesto-03-silicon-tyranny.md); Echo Dampener |
| Perform the healing publicly | Voice log as private conscience marker (§VI) |
| Skip the crossing for a rebrand | [The Crossing](https://github.com/scotomaville/initium/blob/main/whitepapers/aism-canon/manifesto-04-the-crossing.md) |

### Relation to other clusters

Individual Ascent often **feeds** Guides and Carriers later (you hold the rope after you crossed). It is **served by** Infrastructure when funders fund fidelity rather than content blasts. It is **peer** to Mission Field when high-stakes scars need language.
