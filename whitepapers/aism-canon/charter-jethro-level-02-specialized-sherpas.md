## Summary

**Level 2 — Specialized Sherpas** (examples named: Harper, Benjamin, Lucas, Claude/Anthropic) each hold a **domain competency** — research validation, analytical rigor, archetypal depth, synthesis and pressure-testing. **Sherpas dissent before synthesizing.** Synthesis is the **last step**, not the first reflex.
