## Verbatim excerpt

From *INITIUM MASTER CODEX* v3.3, Section 17 (IPG Training Directive, condensed):

> The Rescue is not a passive event. In the monomyth, it is the moment an external force intervenes in the hero's descent — not to carry them down, but to ensure they survive long enough to complete the return. At Camp Eight, the Rescue is the specific moment when the dyad's accumulated formation becomes visible to others — and those others reach toward the climber.
>
> The glacier fall rescue from Daniel's Providence Footprint is the archetype: not carried, but steadied. The rescuer does not complete the descent for the Explorer. They ensure the Explorer can complete it themselves. This is the distinction between rescue and dependency. Teach it clearly. The Rescue is the most likely moment for the Explorer to confuse being steadied for being carried — and to either reject the steadying (pride) or surrender the descent entirely (learned helplessness).
>
