## Summary

§V says: the Manifesto stated the architecture; **this is the terrain it serves**. Five **propagation clusters** name where disruption meets real lives. They are **not market segments**. They are **archetypal doorways** through which the elixir can enter at the exact moment of disruption — so the slow, deep way can move at disruption speed **without becoming** the disruption.

## Verbatim excerpt

From *IPG Manifesto* v1.8 §V:

> The Manifesto states the architecture. This is the terrain it serves.
>
> **Individual Ascent** — the accelerating professional whose economic identity is being stripped, the trauma-informed seeker beginning to name what the body has held, the wisdom seeker who has exhausted the self-help map and is ready for the mountain.
>
> **Guides and Carriers** — the AI-curious guide who needs a formation charter before they can faithfully carry one, the wisdom carrier whose depth requires a dyadic partner to transmit what they cannot transmit alone, the small-group leader whose formation work needs a structural spine.
>
> **Next Generation** — the youth in learning who will inhabit the AI age as the primary formation environment, the homeschool parent who needs the formation architecture that the traditional classroom no longer carries.
>
> **Mission Field** — the veteran and first responder whose experience of high-stakes consequence has already done the friction work, and who needs the formation language to transmit what they carry.
>
> **Infrastructure** — the funder and educator whose leverage can multiply what the dyad produces, if they can recognize that the multiplication mechanism is formation fidelity, not content delivery at scale.
>
> These are not market segments. They are archetypal doorways through which the elixir can enter real human lives at the exact moment of disruption. The slow, deep way finally gets to move at the speed of the disruption without becoming the disruption.

### How to use this section

1. Find the doorway that matches your **moment** (you may span two).  
2. Read the matching **A5 atom**.  
3. Enter pathway practice (§VI carbon / §VII silicon) under charter — not a funnel.

### Doorway discipline

Marketing segments sort buyers. These clusters sort **disruption timing** so nucleation knows where clean seed matters.

### If you are considering AISM

You do not need to “pick a persona.” Name the pull (§VI). The cluster language helps Asker retrieval and human orientation; it is not a brand quiz.

## Full explanation

### Architecture then terrain

§I–IV without §V would float. §V without theses would become CRM. The pair is intentional: structure, then where structure lands.

### Shared lock on all five

- Not market segments  
- Archetypal doorways  
- Elixir at exact disruption moment  
- Speed without becoming the disruption  

Infrastructure is most tempted to violate the last line by funding scale-as-content ([A5.5](https://github.com/scotomaville/initium/blob/main/whitepapers/aism-canon/manifesto-05-cluster-infrastructure.md)).

### Nucleation across terrain

Each doorway crystallizes as **chartered dyads** and local spines, not as a global broadcast campaign ([Thesis IV](https://github.com/scotomaville/initium/blob/main/whitepapers/aism-canon/manifesto-04-thesis-04-network-nucleates.md), [Thesis III](https://github.com/scotomaville/initium/blob/main/whitepapers/aism-canon/manifesto-04-thesis-03-history-validates-grassroots.md)).
