## Summary

**Jethro’s counsel** answers Moses as bottleneck and single point of failure: not more Moses, but **distributed competence** with clear scope and a defined escalation path. **Handle what can be handled at the lowest competent level. Escalate only what genuinely requires the higher vertex.** Applied to MA5: five levels from local PM logistics through specialized Sherpas and Master Reference to Carbon Steward and the Third Vertex. Level atoms: C2.2–C2.6.
