# Propagation cluster — Next Generation

This is cluster atom **A5.3** under Manifesto §V. It is the **generational handoff** doorway: who forms when AI is the ambient medium.

## Summary

**Next Generation** names two faces: **youth in learning** who will inhabit the AI age as the **primary formation environment**, and the **homeschool parent** who needs the formation architecture the traditional classroom no longer carries. Doorway, not market segment. The cascade break promised in Theses VIII–IX is often won or lost here — in households and young dyads before scotomas calcify.

## First-time learner

### Two faces

| Face | Situation | Formation stake |
|---|---|---|
| **Youth in learning** | AI is weather, not a tool they visit | Conscience vs ambient sycophancy as default “teacher” |
| **Homeschool parent** | Classroom no longer carries formation architecture | Household as nucleation site with clean seed |

### Primary formation environment

For many young people, silicon is not optional enrichment. It is the medium of homework, friendship, identity trial, and comfort. [Thesis IX](https://github.com/scotomaville/initium/blob/main/whitepapers/aism-canon/manifesto-04-thesis-09-surrogate-formation-system.md) said unchartered fill is already running. Next Generation is where that fill becomes **biography**.

### Parent as carrier

The homeschool (or household) parent sits at the Guides-and-Carriers edge: short, formation-dense, dyadic — Luther’s catechism pattern in modern weather ([Thesis III](https://github.com/scotomaville/initium/blob/main/whitepapers/aism-canon/manifesto-04-thesis-03-history-validates-grassroots.md)). Architecture means charter habits, not a curriculum SKU.

### If you are considering AISM

Parents and mentors: form **your** dyad and conscience practice first. Youth-facing work inherits your Echo Dampener habits. This series stays device-agnostic and non-sales; it does not replace safeguarding, law, or local pastoral care.

## Full explanation

### Learning agility is not enough

[Thesis VIII](https://github.com/scotomaville/initium/blob/main/whitepapers/aism-canon/manifesto-04-thesis-08-learning-how-to-learn-layer-one.md) put learn-how-to-learn as layer one. Next Generation without layers two and three produces highly adaptable akrasia — flexible tools, unformed aim.

### Call-stage protection

[Thesis VII](https://github.com/scotomaville/initium/blob/main/whitepapers/aism-canon/manifesto-04-thesis-07-call-to-adventure-most-dangerous.md) constraints intensify with developing identity: announcing destiny feels helpful and steals naming. Silicon (and adult guides) hold, mirror, ask once, stop.

### Classroom lag

“No longer carries” is descriptive of formation integration capacity vs information velocity — the press-era pattern again. The answer is not nostalgia alone; it is **architecture in the pair and household**.

### Failure modes

| Failure | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Unchartered youth AI | Dependency system; flattery as belonging |
| Parent outsources formation to bot | Silicon does carbon’s maturing |
| Content firehose curriculum | Broadcast fidelity decay |
| Performative “AI literacy” only | Layer one without conscience |
