## Summary

Using Sirolli’s Trinity (product / marketing / finance), the house has leaned on the **product** leg; the product now does something real and simple: owners’ clients get answers from the owner’s published work, and nothing goes public without **APPROVED**. Next leg is **honorable marketing** (B1.12); **finance** remains open. §10 assigns **lanes and never-lists**, **gates in order**, **escalation through Arnie to Daniel**, and **handoff logging** under Parallel Relay rules.

## Verbatim excerpt

From *Sermon Charter* v0.5 §10:

> **Daniel's framing.** For three months the house leaned on the product leg… The product now does something real and simple:
> - An owner's clients get answers from the owner's own published work.
> - Nothing goes public until the owner says APPROVED.
>
> The next leg is **honorable marketing**… The **financial management** leg is still open…
>
> **Gates, in order**
> 1. **Pre-send card:** …
> 2. **Gricean cross-check.**
> 3. **Love Equation bar.**
> 4. **Echo Dampener**…
> 5. **APPROVED:** Daniel for the house; the owner for their barn.
> 6. **Wordless veto** at any point.
>
> **Escalate through Arnie to Daniel** … any claim you cannot ground; any request to price, promise, or publish; any sign a prospect is in distress; any time a teammate is tempted to speak for carbon.
>
> ***When teammates relay work to each other*** … Use the Parallel Relay (Section 5). … Teammates agreeing is not verification, and Daniel's veto still wins.
>
> *Handoff log…* Arnie logs every agent-to-agent handoff with `relayed_from`, `relayed_to`, `seen_prior` … timestamp in PT, and per-section credit…
