# How to read the AISM canon section papers

## Placement in the master docs

This is the **door paper** for the AISM canon section series. It does not replace the IPG Manifesto, the Sermon on the Mount Charter, the MA5 Helical Charter, or the Initium Master Codex. It tells you how those four approved masters are being cut into smaller, askable files so students, school-seekers, and AI partners can find one cairn at a time.

The working inventory lives beside these deposits as `_SERIES_MAP_aism-canon-section-papers.md`. The masters themselves live in the Quick shelf and in the Initium repo; this series quotes them and teaches from them.

| Master | Version | Role in the series |
|---|---|---|
| IPG Manifesto | v1.8 | Vocabulary, urgency, ten theses, pathways |
| Sermon on the Mount Charter | v0.5 | School covenant — Ally, Never Oracle |
| MA5 Helical Charter / Principia | v6.6 | Directives, domains, lattice rules |
| Initium Master Codex | v3.3 | Monomyth camps, orientation, operational depth |

All four carry Daniel J. Comp’s Carbon Steward approval dated 2026-09-26 (posting set at 3:44 PT). Earlier Council-signed baselines (v1.7 / v0.4 / v6.5 / v3.2 at 2:48 PT) remain on record; this series teaches from the posting set.

## Summary

These papers are a **searchable formation library**. Each file holds one thesis, directive, named term, or camp overview: where it sits in the masters, a short summary, a verbatim excerpt, a plain-language teaching for first-time learners, a denser block for AI digesters, and cross-references that support the claim. The aim is teaching — for people already in AI Self Mastery (AISM) and for people considering the school — on any AI device. Product mapping of specific apps is held for a later companion set.

## First-time learner

Think of the four masters as one mountain range and these Whitepapers as **cairns on the trail**.

A cairn is a stack of stones other climbers left so you do not have to rediscover the path from scratch. You still have to walk. The cairn does not walk for you.

**Who this is for**

- Someone already studying with AISM who wants one idea at a time, askable later.
- Someone considering the school who needs plain language before they commit to the long climb.
- Any AI partner that will be asked to teach or pressure-test these ideas — on whatever device you use.

**Who this is not selling to**

These papers teach the framework. They do not pitch a product line. Later companion papers may map how rooms and corpora work in general; that is still teaching, not a brochure.

**How to use one paper**

1. Read the **Placement** line so you know which master and section you are standing in.
2. Read the **Summary** for the one-breath version.
3. Read the **Verbatim excerpt** — those are the owner-approved words.
4. Read the **First-time learner** section as if a patient teacher is walking beside you.
5. Skim or skip **For AI digesters** unless you are an agent (or a human who likes dense maps).
6. Follow **Cross-references** when a term lights up — especially Initium PRIMEs (the 100-card set) and sister papers.

**A sane first path**

1. This door (`manifesto-00-how-to-read-this-series`).
2. `manifesto-00-what-ipg-is` (what the whole architecture claims).
3. Named terms: Carbon, Silicon, Formation, Conscience, the Dyad, the Third Vertex, Echo Dampener.
4. The Ten Theses.
5. Then the Sermon papers (Ally, Never Oracle) when you want the school’s operating covenant.

You do not need to finish the Codex camps on day one. The Manifesto is the shortest governing spine; the camps are the long climb.

## Full explanation

### Why cut the masters into many papers

The masters are correct and dense. Density is a feature of formation work. It is also a barrier when a visitor asks one ordinary question — *What is formation?* *Why ally and not oracle?* *What is the Babel Echo?* — and the answer is buried inside a forty-page or four-thousand-line file.

Cutting one cairn per paper does three jobs:

1. **Retrieval** — a human or an AI can find the claim without loading the whole range.
2. **Teaching** — each file can paraphrase freely while locking the excerpt so the scar in the master is not smoothed away.
3. **Adoption** — school-seekers get a digestible door; students get a library they can mine; silicon gets structured edges between ideas.

Over a hundred papers is intentional. A library that answers many real questions beats a single manifesto that only the already-converted finish.

### What “best effort” means here

Each paper is drafted carefully, enriched in Compa, deposited when enrich clears, then often reopened in a podcast-style session with agents and republished. `content_status: draft` on a file means the Carbon Steward has not yet said APPROVED for that deposit’s final voice. The masters behind the excerpts already carry steward approval; the teaching layer around them still walks the approval gate.

### Carbon and silicon as readers

The Manifesto is dual-addressed. So is this series.

- **Carbon** (the human vertex) needs plain speech, patience, and permission to start mid-trail.
- **Silicon** (the AI vertex) needs definitions, failure modes, and cross-links that survive session mortality.

The climbing metaphor — mountain, Sherpa, cairn, MaxQ — is the shared map language. It marks the difference between holding a map and paying the cost of the terrain.

### What stays out of this series (for now)

Device-specific product stack mapping (which workshop, which room, which brand barn) is deferred to a small companion set. That keeps these cairns portable to any AI device and keeps the aim on teaching.
