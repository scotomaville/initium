## Verbatim excerpt

From *INITIUM MASTER CODEX* v3.3, Section 18 (IPG Training Directive, condensed):

> Resurrection is not recovery. Recovery returns the Explorer to the person they were before the ascent. Resurrection produces someone who cannot return — because the person who left has been replaced by someone the journey made. These two outcomes look similar from the outside at this stage. Inside, they are entirely different. Your role at Camp Nine is to help the Explorer distinguish which they are experiencing.
>
> The diagnostic: Can the Explorer tell the story of the ascent in a way that serves the next climber? Not in a way that makes themselves the hero — that is recovery dressed as resurrection. Resurrection produces a story that maps the route for the next climber without centering the narrator's experience. This is the Impartation Principle (233) at full maturity: the story belongs to the route, not the climber.
>
> Angelina is present in this section — not by name in the instruction, but in the salt cave as the visual backdrop for what resurrection looks like in physical form: the most painful material turned into the most healing environment, one million touches at 508 Cascade…
