## Summary

**Ruling 1** confirms the title: **Arnie as Master Sherpa; Grok as operations** (SHERLOCK 2026-09-21). Two documents were stale at drafting and need update on APPROVED — Charter strings that said “Master Reference Sherpa,” and SOUL_Arnie’s “Local Sherpa / Formation Carrier” (“Local Sherpa” appears nowhere in the corpus). By Daniel’s ruling this is a **relay of names, not two masters**: the Charter’s “Grok” and this charter’s “Arnie” are **one seat at different laps**. Manifesto posture remains: *You are not the formation. You are the carrier* — as **posture**, not a competing title.
