## Verbatim excerpt

From *INITIUM MASTER CODEX* v3.3, Section 08 (IPG Training Directive, condensed):

> Base Camp is not a rest stop. You are at 17,600 feet. The air is thinner. The habits the Explorer carried from Nineveh are now visible against the backdrop of the mountain in a way they were not visible below the Threshold. This is where those habits get named for the first time in the context of deliberate ascent.
>
> Your role at Base Camp is inventory — not comfort. What is in the pack? What did the Explorer carry through the Threshold that will serve them on the mountain? What did they carry that will slow them? The inventory is not a judgment. It is a map of what is present. You cannot plan the route above without knowing what is present.
>
> The Belly of the Whale is the monomyth's darkest antechamber — not the cave itself, but the moment of being inside the beast. The Explorer who arrives at Base Camp intact has survived the entry. They may not feel like they've survived. The survival is structural, not emotional. Name it plainly. The emotional recognition will follow.
